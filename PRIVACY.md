# SavorLoop Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

SavorLoop is operated by Luka Djordjevic. This policy describes the initial free iPhone app and its public GitHub support repository.

## Information processed on your iPhone

SavorLoop uses the information you enter to create and personalize meal plans. This may include your goals, age, body measurements, nutrition targets, allergies, dietary restrictions, food preferences, kitchen equipment and schedule. Saved meals, plans, meal ratings, inferred preferences, grocery and preparation checkoffs, and reminder settings are stored locally in the app.

SavorLoop does not require an account and does not operate a server that receives your profile or meal history. Meal planning and personalization run on your device. The app does not send your nutrition information to a remote language-model service. Optional on-device language features depend on Apple's device, settings and model availability. Apple manages its own system services.

The food reference library is bundled with the app. Food searches and manually entered barcodes are processed locally. Opening a source or license link takes you to an external website governed by that provider's privacy policy.

## Advertising, tracking and payments

The initial release is free, with no enabled in-app purchases. SavorLoop does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking or a custom analytics upload service. We do not sell or rent your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Apple independently processes App Store downloads, Apple account information and system diagnostics under its own policies. Depending on your Apple settings, Apple may make crash, performance or usage reports available to the developer. Such reports, if provided, are used to understand reliability and improve the app, not for advertising. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Your choices, storage and deletion

In **You → Privacy & data**, you can export your app data or choose **Delete all local data**. Local data remains until you delete it or remove the app. The app configures its personal database for iOS file protection and exclusion from device backups; it does not provide cloud synchronization or a developer-managed backup.

Exporting creates a file that may contain sensitive personal information. You choose whether and where to share it using the system share sheet. Copies you save or share are controlled by you and the destination service. Deleting local app data does not delete exported copies, information you posted to GitHub, or Apple's records. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

Reminders are optional local notifications. You can disable them in the app or in iOS notification settings.

## GitHub support and public information

Support is available through the [SavorLoop community repository](https://github.com/lukaluka/savorloop-community) and its [issue tracker](https://github.com/lukaluka/savorloop-community/issues).

GitHub is a separate service. Visiting or using it may involve GitHub processing your account details, IP address, cookies and usage information under the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

If you open an issue or comment, your GitHub username and the content you submit are public and can be viewed by the developer and others. We use support submissions to answer questions, investigate problems and improve SavorLoop. Public issues may remain as a support record after they are closed; closing an issue does not delete it. GitHub controls its own retention, backups and account settings. We cannot remove copies retained by other people, search engines or services.

**Do not post health information, body measurements, allergies, meal histories, personal exports, passwords or other sensitive information in public issues.** Describe technical problems using non-sensitive examples. For a privacy request, open an issue asking for a private contact route without including the underlying private information.

## Adults and children

SavorLoop is intended for adults aged 18 and older. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly solicit children's personal information through support. If you believe a child has posted personal information in the community repository, request removal without repeating that information publicly.

## Privacy questions and requests

For questions about this policy, or requests concerning information you provided through support, contact Luka Djordjevic through the [community issue tracker](https://github.com/lukaluka/savorloop-community/issues). Do not include sensitive information in the initial public request. Requests about data stored only on your iPhone can be handled using the app's export and deletion controls; the developer cannot access that local database remotely.

## Changes

We will update this page when the app's privacy practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy. Changes that introduce new collection or sharing will be reflected in the policy and applicable App Store disclosures.
