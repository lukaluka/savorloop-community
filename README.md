# SavorLoop support

**Meals that learn you.**

SavorLoop is an iPhone meal planner that brings meal plans, groceries, preparation and meal feedback together. Planning and personalization run on your device.

## Get help or report a problem

[Open a support issue](https://github.com/lukaluka/savorloop-community/issues/new), or [browse existing issues](https://github.com/lukaluka/savorloop-community/issues).

Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, the steps that led to the problem, and what you expected to happen. GitHub requires an account to post.

**Issues and comments are public.** Do not include health information, body measurements, allergies, meal histories, personal data exports, passwords or other sensitive information. Remove personal information from screenshots. Use made-up examples when describing a problem.

## Privacy and your data

Read the [SavorLoop Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

In the app, go to **You → Privacy & data** to export your data or delete all local data. Deleting local data does not delete copies you previously exported or posted elsewhere. No SavorLoop sign-in is required.

For privacy questions, open an issue with a general request for a private contact route; do not post the private information itself.

## About the initial release

The initial launch is planned as a free app in the United States. This repository is the support and policy site; its existence does not mean the app is already available on the App Store.

SavorLoop is intended for adults. Nutrition estimates and meal plans are general planning information, not medical care. Always check ingredients and food labels for your needs.
