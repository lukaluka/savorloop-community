## A simple weekly routine

1. Set your preferences and create a plan.
2. Review the meals, ingredient amounts, and planning notes before shopping.
3. Open **Groceries** and check items as you get them.
4. Open **Prep** for the preparation tasks and available timers.
5. Open a meal to see its instructions, record that you ate it, or rate it.
6. Create the next week using the feedback you have saved.

The main tabs are **Today**, **Plan**, **Groceries**, **Prep**, and **You**. Plans, portions, groceries, and prep come from the same saved plan. Your explicit exclusions take priority over preference learning.

## Get help safely

GitHub issues are public and serve as our support tickets. A GitHub account is needed to open or comment on a ticket. Reading these guides does not require one.

Use fictional examples. Do **not** post your nutrition export, health history, body measurements, allergy profile, passwords, payment information, receipts, or personal contact details. Review screenshots carefully. For a suspected security vulnerability or unintended exposure of data, use [private vulnerability reporting](SECURITY.md), not a public issue. We currently do not offer a separate private general-support inbox.

SavorLoop is a consumer meal-planning tool for adults. Its estimates and filters are not medical care or a guarantee of allergy safety. See the [product safety boundaries](guides/preferences.md). Community support cannot provide clinical advice or emergency assistance.
