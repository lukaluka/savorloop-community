# Plans, portions, and meals

[Guide index](../README.md) · [Feedback](feedback.md) · [Groceries and prep](groceries-and-prep.md)

## Read Today and the weekly plan

**Today** shows the selected day's meals and planned nutrition. Tap a meal card or **See your meal** for its details. **View week** opens the weekly plan. The selected day can be changed in **Plan** using the horizontal day selector.

In **Plan**, review the meals for each day and open **Planning notes** when it is available. Notes can explain limitations or compromises in an otherwise eligible plan. They are useful context when a result differs from your expectations.

If there is no current plan but your preferences are saved, choose **Create my plan**. If you see a generation progress card, let the operation complete before trying to generate another plan.

## Read a meal

A meal screen includes the title and summary, an estimated cooking time, planned portion nutrition, **What you'll need**, **Let's make it**, and required equipment when applicable.

Ingredient amounts in the meal screen are for your planned portion. They may be fractional gram amounts because the portion is calculated from the underlying recipe. The household grocery list can therefore show larger totals than a single meal screen.

Read whether the ingredient is raw, dry, cooked, or ready to eat. Those states affect both the quantity and the nutrient reference. Do not assume that the weight of a cooked ingredient is a raw purchase weight or that swapping the food state preserves the displayed nutrition.

## Understand planned nutrition

The summary presents calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, and fiber for the planned meal or day. Where a target is available, the app also shows the planning target. These are estimates from the listed ingredient quantities and reference data.

Planned nutrition is not a complete consumed-food log. Dinners designated as meals out are omitted because their nutrition is unknown. **Skipped** and **Ate out** record behavior; they do not turn the app into a verified intake tracker.

If a saved plan's recipe or nutrition cannot be resolved against the installed catalog, the app may show an unavailable message instead of inventing a total. Include the message and a fictional reproduction in a support ticket.

## Swap a meal

1. Open the meal you want to change.
2. Choose **Swap meal**.
3. Review the eligible alternatives shown by the app.
4. Choose an alternative and return to the current plan.
5. Recheck the meal's ingredients, nutrition, groceries, and prep after the replacement.

There may be no suitable alternative for a restrictive combination. A swap must continue to honor firm constraints. This control replaces a planned meal; it is not an arbitrary ingredient editor or a way to add every food-library record to the planner.

## Rebuild this week or create the next week

**Rebuild this week** generates the current planning period again using the latest saved preferences and feedback. Use it when your current week needs to reflect a changed preference or exclusion. Review regenerated lists and tasks rather than assuming every earlier checkoff still describes the new plan.

**Create next week** asks for confirmation and generates a subsequent week from your latest preferences. Existing saved plan records remain on the device. The current interface is centered on the active plan; retaining history does not imply a full history-browsing or import feature.

Changing preferences through **You** also offers **Save and rebuild plan**. A **Never again** rating can deactivate an active week that contains the excluded meal. See [feedback](feedback.md) for the recovery path.

## When a plan is unavailable after a change

The app rechecks a restored plan against current eligibility, restrictions, and installed reference data. If an older active plan no longer qualifies, it may be deactivated while its historical record is retained. Read the message and create a fresh plan using supported preferences. Do not delete your local data as an initial troubleshooting step.

## Recipe collection in development

The expanded development collection contains 250 recipes. Availability in your installed version may differ; this is not a release announcement. Your equipment, cooking-time limit, allergies and exclusions determine which meals can appear. Recipes shared by lunch and dinner count once, and portion changes do not count as additional recipes.

Recipes listing cooked grains or legumes use already-cooked weights. Their time estimates assume those ingredients are prepared and safely refrigerated or purchased ready-cooked; cooking them from dry adds time. Follow the ingredient state and preparation instructions.

A food-reference version update can ask you to rebuild the active week. Existing history is retained; the update does not rewrite old meal quantities.


## Free and Plus in development

The updated development build includes Free daily personalized planning, the complete recipe collection and taste learning. SavorLoop Plus is designed for seven-day plans with consolidated groceries and prep. The next candidate enables Apple subscription offers when available. Subscriptions still require Apple approval and release; this is not a release or purchase announcement. If offers cannot load, Free daily planning remains available.

Saved plans remain on your iPhone when Plus ends, including their groceries, prep, checklists and safe meal swaps. Open Saved plans from Plan to view history; reopening still checks current restrictions and the food reference version. Older or newly excluded plans may remain historical records rather than active recommendations. Export keeps your saved history.

Original weekly access is checked through Apple's verified app purchase record. Use Restore purchases on the Plus screen after reinstalling with the same Apple account. Local personal data remains excluded from backups and is not restored by purchase restoration. Deleting data does not cancel Apple subscriptions.

Explore recipes from Plan to browse the collection with Free. Browsing includes recipes that may conflict with your preferences, restrictions or equipment; review ingredient labels and instructions before cooking. Personalized planning performs the eligibility checks.
