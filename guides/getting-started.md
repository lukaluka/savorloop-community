# Getting started

[Guide index](../README.md) · [Get help](../SUPPORT.md)

These steps describe the current development build. Check [availability](availability.md) before looking for an installation. There is no app installer in this repository, and opening a support ticket does not enroll you in a test program.

## What SavorLoop does

SavorLoop brings a meal plan, ingredient quantities, groceries, preparation tasks, and meal feedback together on your iPhone. You describe your preferences and practical constraints, review the resulting plan, and give feedback as you use it. Your saved feedback helps inform subsequent plans.

You do not create a SavorLoop account in the current build. Core planning uses local data. A connection to GitHub is needed only when you choose to visit this community or send a ticket; posting here is separate from using the app.

## First launch: four steps

1. **Choose your focus.** Pick the goal that best describes what you want from meal planning. Use **Continue** to move forward. A preference can be changed later.
2. **Review your starting point.** The app asks for the inputs used in its adult planning estimate. If you already have appropriate targets, optional calorie and protein fields are available. The fields are validated; an invalid entry is not silently accepted. Do not use a sample target from a screenshot as your own recommendation.
3. **Tell the app about your tastes and restrictions.** Select allergies and dietary preferences, then review foods you love, dislike, or exclude. Add an unsupported strict restriction in the provided field rather than leaving out a requirement to obtain a plan. The app may stop and explain that its catalog cannot meet your needs.
4. **Describe your kitchen and week.** Choose equipment, household size, cooking time, repetition preference, leftovers, and any planned dinners out. The optional grocery budget is a stored preference; it is not a live price quote.

Tap **Make my first plan**. Wait for planning to finish, then review the plan and any messages. If the app cannot create a plan, use [plan troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md). Do not weaken an allergy or other firm restriction just to make planning succeed.

Use **Back** within setup to revisit an earlier page. After setup, open **You → Edit preferences** and finish with **Save and rebuild plan** to apply changes.

## Know the five tabs

| Tab | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | The selected day's meals, planned nutrition, and shortcuts into your week and prep |
| **Plan** | Choosing a day, reviewing meals and planning notes, rebuilding, or creating the next week |
| **Groceries** | Ingredient quantities grouped by aisle, checkoffs, and the meals using each ingredient |
| **Prep** | Preparation tasks, completion state, available timers, and keeping the screen awake |
| **You** | Preferences, learning signals, exclusions, reminders, data controls, and the food library |

## Before your first shop

Open the individual meals and read the ingredient names and food states. A cooked quantity is not automatically the same amount to buy raw. Review the household size in Groceries. The meal screen describes your planned portion; the grocery list covers the configured household.

A planned meal out has unknown nutrition and is not included in the app's planned totals. A daily total describes what the plan contains, not a complete record of everything you actually ate.

## After a meal

Open the meal and choose **I ate this** or **Rate meal**. Optional reasons can make your feedback more informative. **Never again** is a firm recipe exclusion and can require rebuilding an active week. Read [feedback and personalization](feedback.md) before using it as a simple “less often” rating.
