# Community guidelines

This space is for constructive help with SavorLoop. Treat people respectfully, explain disagreements with evidence, and welcome users with different levels of technical knowledge and accessibility needs.

Please:

- Discuss the behavior or documentation, not someone's personal characteristics.
- Use clear, relevant titles and keep follow-ups in the existing ticket.
- Protect your own and other people's private information.
- Share reproducible examples and distinguish observations from assumptions.
- Respect a person's decision not to disclose health, identity, or other personal details.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual content, doxxing, impersonation, spam, malicious links, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Do not offer diagnoses, prescribe targets to other users, solicit sensitive health records, or claim to represent the maintainers without authorization.

Maintainers may edit or remove unsafe content where GitHub permits, lock discussions, close off-topic threads, or limit participation. Moderation should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact.

Use GitHub's **Report content** or user-reporting controls for abusive material. Avoid publicly repeating the harmful content or exposing someone's identity while reporting it. A general private moderation inbox is not currently offered. Security vulnerabilities and unintended information exposure use the [private security channel](SECURITY.md).

These guidelines apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, and other project-hosted interactions. They do not promise continuous monitoring or emergency assistance.
